Tetris Prime — announcer voice clips
==========================================

182 clips = 168 REAL "Raffina" recordings + 14 neural fallback (v4.11.0)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  PRIMARY (168 clips): REAL fish.audio recordings of "Raffina
           (Puyo Puyo Tetris)" — an energetic young female commentator
           voice (model by Curedoremi2009 on fish.audio).
           Source: the game owner's batch recordings (10 files, one per
           category, multiple phrases per file).
           Pipeline: tools/raffina_vosk.py (offline word timestamps)
             -> raffina_align.py (DP token alignment to expected phrases)
             -> raffina_cut.py (ffmpeg loudnorm I=-14 LUFS / TP=-1.5,
                highpass 70Hz, 0.08s fade-out, mono 44.1 kHz, 96 kbps mp3)
           Events: start, clear1, clear2, clear3, tetris, tspin, pc, b2b,
           combo2..combo10, levelup, danger, saved, score10k..score1m,
           lines25..lines200
           Quality: every cut validated (duration/loudness/size) + a diverse
           sample verified by speech-to-text ("Back-to-back Tetris!",
           "Absolutely massive!", "Perfect clear!", "Level five, halfway",
           "One million points, historic!", ...).

  FALLBACK (14 clips): Microsoft Edge neural TTS (free, no key)
           Voice:   en-US-AvaNeural (young female), rate +20% / pitch +10Hz
           Events:  record, near, over, win, timeup, hurry (not present in
                    the Raffina batch recordings yet) + combo9 (the Raffina
                    cut turned out silent; regenerated in v4.11.0)
           Script:  tools/generate-edge-voices.ts

File naming: {event}-{variant}.mp3 — index order matches VOICE_MAP in
src/lib/tetris/audio.ts 1:1 (order matters!).
